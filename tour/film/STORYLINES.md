# Storylines: the Loadout tour

Recommended: **A**. A stranger follows one real store order from start to finish, which is exactly what a tutorial must teach.

## A. One Friday order (picked)
- Device: the worked example. One real order (the Grocery Friday preset: 306 cases) travels from a plain load sheet into Loadout and comes out as five built pallets.
- Form: one continuous take. The camera glides over the real app, framing each part as the cursor uses it; captions name each step.
- First two seconds: the load sheet already printing on the warehouse-dark floor, "Friday's order." beside it.
- Beats: load sheet → "How does it fit on pallets?" → the sheet carries into the app and lands on the Grocery Friday button → click, the pallet builds → limits (height, weight) → tabs, another pallet builds → layers x-ray → chips ride on top → change a number, it rebuilds → add your own product → "Your order, built into pallets."
- Look: the app's own: ground #0b0b0c, halftone floor, safety orange #ff5b1f rationed, Geist / Geist Mono.
- Sound: calm minimal electronic bed ~100 bpm; soft clicks on real clicks, keys under typing, one whoosh on the carry-across.

## B. The loader's rules
- Device: type carries the argument; form: one frame that rebuilds. Each rule is one big line (heavy low · chips on top · nothing hangs over air · every pallet square) while the 3D pallet rebuilds beside it to prove it, then a short how-to.

## C. Result first
- Device: the result first; form: a pull-back. Opens on the satisfying build, pulls back to the order that made it, then three verbs: pick, check, add.
